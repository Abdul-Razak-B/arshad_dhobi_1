THE ATELIER PHILOSOPHY

Slow Craftsmanship in an Age of Haste.

Conventional commercial dry cleaning relies on aggressive petrochemicals and high-heat mechanical presses that fatigue delicate weaves and dull natural luster.

At Arshad Dhobi, every garment is treated as a singular commission. Our master textile custodians map stain chemistry by hand, immerse fabrics in temperature-calibrated botanical emulsions, and finish collars, lapels, and embroidery using weighted cedar-steam irons.

Your wardrobe returns resting on contoured beechwood hangers, sealed inside breathable, unbleached organic cotton dust covers—never suffocating plastic.

THE PROTOCOL

Four Rituals of Uncompromising Care.

01.

White-Glove Collection & Garment Inspection

Our uniformed valet collects your wardrobe in garment-grade carriers. At the atelier, master custodians examine seam tension, buttons, and stain composition under optical magnification.

02.

Bespoke Treatment Bath

Garments are separated by fiber weight and dye fastness, then cleansed in purified, mineral-balanced botanical baths tailored specifically to silk, cashmere, wool, or hand-loomed cotton.

03.

Artisanal Hand-Pressing

Using traditional heavy brass irons and controlled dry-steam tables, artisans sculpt lapel rolls, crisp cuffs, and delicate pleats without crushing embroidered motifs or metallic zari threadwork.

04.

Breathable Packaging & Delivery

Each piece passes a final 12-point quality inspection before being draped over contoured wooden hangers, tissue-padded, and delivered inside breathable cotton garment bags.
